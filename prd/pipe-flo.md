# PIPE-FLO Connect

## What & Why

Vision & Strategy

PIPE-FLO Connect brings PIPE-FLO's established fluid-system modeling capabilities to a modern cloud platform.

The strategy is to preserve customers' existing engineering work while adding AI-assisted model creation, browser-based access, real-time collaboration, 3D visualization, and direct migration of existing PIPE-FLO Desktop models.

**Success:** Engineering teams build models faster, collaborate without file handoffs or version confusion, and move from Desktop to Connect without rebuilding their existing models.
