# SpecPage

## What & Why

Vision & Strategy

SpecPage's strategy is to move food-and-beverage product-data management toward a modern browser-native platform with stronger supplier collaboration, sustainability support, AI-assisted insights, analytics, and global scalability.

SpecPage 2.0 is positioned as a connected replacement for spreadsheet-driven specification and formulation work, with compliance, data management, formulation, and publishing handled in one environment.

**Success:** Customers can manage product data, recipes, specifications, compliance, supplier information, and publishing with less manual work and lower implementation cost.
