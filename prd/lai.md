# LAI

## What & Why

Vision & Strategy

AGi32 Illuminate modernizes AGi32 while preserving its trusted photometric accuracy. The goal is to reduce repetitive manual workflows, improve project setup and editing, modernize compliance validation, and make professional lighting-design workflows faster and easier to scale.

The strategy is to launch Illuminate for new customers and progressively migrate the installed AGi32 base while maintaining continuity for existing users.

**Success:** Faster design and iteration, continued AGi32-level accuracy, strong adoption of modern editing tools, improved user satisfaction, and eventual adoption of AI-assisted workflows.
