# PRO.FILE

## What & Why

Vision & Strategy

PRO.FILE is intended to remain a secure system of record for product data while connecting people, processes, and enterprise systems across the product lifecycle. The strategy combines hybrid deployment, stronger security and governance, web access, modern integrations, and practical AI.

Customers should be able to modernize incrementally across on-premises, cloud, and mixed environments without sacrificing control or continuity.

**Success:** PRO.FILE evolves from traditional product-data management toward a connected, hybrid PLM platform with better search, stronger governance, modern integrations, and assistive AI.
