# FlowIQ (Chimera)

## What & Why

Vision & Strategy

FlowIQ is an intelligent pump-selection, configuration, and quoting application for both technical and non-technical users.

The experience begins with application requirements such as flow, head, fluid type, efficiency, NPSHr, speed, and impeller diameter. A performance-driven engineering calculation engine evaluates pump-curve data and returns a ranked list of technically acceptable pumps.

After selecting a pump, users move into a guided configuration workflow where available choices are dynamically filtered. Changes to motors, seals, materials, coatings, and other technical attributes automatically recalculate pump performance, ensuring the completed configuration remains valid for the application.

The strategy is to replace disconnected selection, configuration, and quoting tools with one integrated workflow for manufacturers, distributors, dealers, engineers, and sales teams.

**Success:**
- Complete selection → configuration → quote in under 5 minutes
- Support 100% of valid product configurations without manual intervention
- Reduce internal sales time-to-quote by 50%
- Achieve at least 85% user satisfaction
- Keep post-sale corrections caused by selection or configuration errors below 2%
- Increase quote-to-order conversion by at least 15%
- Expand adoption to at least 3 global regions within the first 12 months
- Maintain 99.9% system availability

## Release Horizon

Release Planning

**NOW · Selection and Product Administration**
- Capture application and technical requirements
- Manage product lines, pump sizes, curves, and catalog trims
- Match requirements against catalog and pump-curve data
- Return ranked, technically valid recommendations
- Resolve core admin navigation and product-data relationships

**NEXT · Configuration and Performance Validation**
- Guide users through valid pump configurations
- Dynamically filter motors, seals, materials, coatings, and related options
- Recalculate performance as configuration changes
- Compare pumps and visualize performance curves
- Validate BEP, NPSH, efficiency, and other operating conditions

**LATER · Quoting and Connected Workflows**
- Generate detailed quotes with performance curves, configuration specifications, pricing, and lead times
- Support approval and version-control workflows
- Integrate inventory and pricing with ERP systems
- Integrate quote tracking with CRM systems
- Support multi-currency, regional certifications, and international operation
- Add analytics, feedback loops, monitoring, and optimization tools
