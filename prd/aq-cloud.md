# AQ Cloud

## What & Why

Vision & Strategy

AQ Cloud moves AutoQuotes from a desktop application to a cloud-first platform accessible through modern browsers and devices. The broader vision combines cloud quoting, connected product and pricing data, AI-assisted quoting, and ERP/CRM integration.

The strategy is phased: establish browser-based catalog access, expand into projects and quotes, then add deeper configuration, AI, analytics, and connected order workflows.

**Success:** Dealers and reps can research products, manage projects, create and share quotes, collaborate, and connect quoting to ordering without depending on the desktop application.
