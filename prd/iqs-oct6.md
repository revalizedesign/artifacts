# Intelliquip Select

## What & Why

Vision & Strategy

IQ Select is the next generation of online pump selection—bringing Intelliquip’s legacy selection experience from the 2010s into the 2020s.

Our strategy is to migrate customers from legacy products to IQ Select by making the new experience compelling enough that they choose to move, rather than forcing migration.

**Success:** Customers are impressed, want to move to IQ Select, and don't have a negative reaction to the new experience.
